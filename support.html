Support

Cue coaches how you say your script, then follows your voice while you practise on camera.

Need help?

  • Open Cue, tap the menu at the top of Scripts, then Contact Support. It opens an email to us.
  • Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Coaching

The marks don't appear. Coaching needs an internet connection. Without one, Cue gives you quick marks on the phone; tap Try AI coaching when you're back online. Scripts must be under the length limit shown under the editor.

I edited my script. Tap Update coaching to coach the new text. Coaching only changes when the words do.

Practice

The prompter doesn't follow my voice. Check that Cue has microphone access in Settings. The first time, Cue downloads its voice model (about 150 MB), so give it a minute on Wi-Fi. You can switch to Auto to scroll at a set pace instead.

The camera is black. Allow camera access in Settings → Cue.

Takes and reviews

Where are my takes? In the Takes tab, and under each script's Takes tab. Cue keeps your best take, starred takes and your last five per script.

Saving to Photos. Open a take, tap •••, then Save to Photos. Takes saved only in Cue are deleted if you delete the app.

A take says it couldn't be reviewed. The voice model may still be downloading. Open the take and tap Try again.
