Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version

  • Your videos and your voice never leave your phone.
  • Only your script's text is sent to our coaching service, to work out how to say it.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your takes. Video and sound recorded in Talkr are stored inside the app on your phone. They are only copied to your Photos library when you choose "Save to Photos", and only shared when you choose "Share".

Your voice. Talkr follows your voice while you practise and reviews each take (what you said, your pace, your pauses and filler words) using a speech model that runs on your phone. Your audio is not uploaded to us.

Your scripts, scores and answers. Scripts, reviews, practice history and the answers you give during setup (what you practise for, how you feel on camera) are stored on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent off your phone

Script text, for coaching. When you ask Talkr to coach a script, the words of that script are sent to our coaching service, which runs on Convex, and processed by OpenAI to suggest which words to stress and where to pause. We keep the coaching result on our service so the same script is coached instantly next time. It is stored against a fingerprint of the text, not against you. We don't ask for your name, email or any account. OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models by default.

The voice model download. The first time you open Talkr, it downloads the speech model from Hugging Face. This is an ordinary download; nothing about you is sent with it.

Apple speech recognition, as a backup. If the on-phone model isn't ready yet, Talkr may briefly use Apple's speech recognition to follow your voice. Apple recognizes speech on your phone where it can; otherwise, audio may be processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Camera, to show and record you while you practise.
  • Microphone, to record sound and follow your voice.
  • Speech recognition, for the Apple backup described above.
  • Photos (add only), asked only when you save a take. Talkr can't see your library.

You can change any of these in the Settings app.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or build a profile of you. Talkr is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a take or script in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your phone. Coaching results on our service hold no personal details; if you'd like a script's coaching removed, contact us with its text.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions? See Support, or use Contact Support in the app's menu.
